# Musk Tweet Analysis Reference

## Data Sources for Prediction
- Elon Musk's X/Twitter posting history
- Tesla earnings calls, product announcements
- SpaceX launch schedules
- DOGE (Department of Government Efficiency) activity
- US political news cycle
- Major tech industry events

## Historical Patterns
- **Earnings weeks**: Musk typically posts more during Tesla earnings
- **Launch weeks**: SpaceX Starship tests correlate with 20-30% more posting
- **Political controversy**: DOGE criticism, congressional hearings = spike in posts
- **Quiet periods**: Holiday weeks, international travel = fewer posts
- **Weekend vs weekday**: Slightly more active on weekdays

## Key Indicators for This Week
When analyzing the current week, consider:
1. Is there a Tesla/SpaceX event scheduled?
2. Is there political controversy involving Musk/DOGE?
3. Is Musk traveling or at a conference?
4. What was last week's count? (momentum)
5. Are there any legal proceedings or regulatory events?

## Pricing Guide
- Ranges near the expected count trade at 15-40¢
- Adjacent ranges trade at 5-15¢
- Extreme ranges (very low/high) trade at 1-5¢
- The edge comes from buying underpriced adjacent ranges
